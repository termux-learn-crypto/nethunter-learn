## Social Engineering Kya Hai?

Social engineering ek aisa attack hai jisme hacker technology nahi, balki insaan ki psychology ko target karta hai. Ye kisi bhi technical vulnerability se zyada dangerous ho sakta hai kyunki systems toh patched ho sakte hain, lekin insaan nahi.

## Why It Works

Social engineering kaam karti hai kyunki:
- **Trust**: Log naturally trustworthy hote hain
- **Fear**: Dar dikhakar informaton nikalna
- **Urgency**: Jaldbaazi mein log galati kar dete hain
- **Authority**: Boss ya senior bankar puchna
- **Greed**: Mufft ka offer dekar fasana

## Common Attack Types

### 1. Phishing

Sabse common social engineering attack. Nakli email ya message bhejkar sensitive info nikalna.

```
Example: "Aapka bank account block ho gaya hai. 
Yahan click karein aur login karein: https://malicious-link.com"
```

### 2. Vishing (Voice Phishing)

Phone call karke personal info nikalna:
- "Main XYZ bank se bol raha hoon. Aapka ATM card block ho gaya hai."
- "Aapka Aadhaar link nahi hai. OTP batao."

### 3. Smishing (SMS Phishing)

SMS ke through phishing:
```
"Congrats! Aapne ₹1,00,000 jeete hain. Claim karne ke liye yahan click karein:"
```

### 4. Pretexting

Ek fake scenario bana ke info nikalna:
- "Sir, main IT department se hoon. Server upgrade ho raha hai. Aapka password batao."
- "Mujhe aapka bank statement chahiye investigation ke liye."

### 5. Baiting

Lalach dekar fasana. Jaise ke:
- USB drive parking lot mein chodh dena
- Free movie download ka link bhejna
- Fake software crack offer karna

### 6. Tailgating

Authorized person ke peeche restricted area mein ghus jaana.

### 7. Quid Pro Quo

Kuch dekar badle me kuch lena:
- "Free website security check karenge?"
- "Free virus scan karein?"

## Real-World Examples

### The Twitter Hack (2020)
Hackers ne Twitter employees ko social engineering kiya aur 130+ celebrities ke accounts hack kar liye.

### Google & Facebook Scam ($100M)
Ek hacker ne fake invoices bhejkar Google aur Facebook se $100 million dollar scam kar liye.

### Target Breach (2013)
HVAC contractor ko targeted kiya aur Target ke 40 million customers ka data leak kiya.

## Kaise Pehchane

Social engineering attack ke red flags:

1. **Urgency**: "Abhi karo, warna account block ho jayega"
2. **Unfamiliar sender**: Unknown email ya number
3. **Grammar mistakes**: Official emails me spelling mistakes
4. **Suspicious links**: Link ka URL carefully check karein
5. **OTP sharing**: Koi bhi OTP kabhi na batayein
6. **Too good to be true**: Free offers, lottery winnings

## Prevention Tips

1. **Verify identity**: Phone pe koi manager bhi bole toh verify karein
2. **Don't share OTP**: Kabhi kisi ko bhi OTP na batao
3. **Use 2FA**: Two-factor authentication everywhere
4. **Check URLs**: Link pe click karne se pehle URL check karein
5. **Be suspicious**: Koi bhi unknown request ho toh suspicious rahein
6. **Report**: Phishing attempts report karein
7. **Update software**: Systems updated rakkhein
8. **Parcel scams**: "Courier aapka hai" wale scams se bachein

## Conclusion

Social engineering kaafi dangerous hota hai kyunki ye technology ke bajay insaan ko target karta hai. Stay alert, stay safe. Koi bhi urgent request ho toh pehle verify karein.
